Our Story

Built by students, for change

Zephyra was born inside a college classroom — not a corporate boardroom. We are a team of AIML undergraduate students who looked at the world around us and decided we didn't want to wait for someone else to fix it.

We built this platform because we believe young people are not the problem — they are the solution. Zephyra is our attempt to channel that energy, curiosity, and urgency into real, measurable action.

From coding late into the night to cold-emailing NGOs and designing challenges around real environmental issues — every bit of Zephyra was put together by people who genuinely care. We are not a startup. We are not funded. We are just students who wanted to make a difference among young minds.

And we're just getting started. 🌱

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Zephyra is a student-built project. We are committed to being transparent about the data we collect and how we use it.

What We Collect

  • Basic account information: name, email address, institution name, and grade.
  • Activity data: challenges completed, points earned, and eco logs submitted.
  • Usage data: pages visited, time spent on the platform (anonymous analytics only).

How We Use Your Data

  • To operate your account and display your progress.
  • To calculate and display leaderboard rankings.
  • To send platform-related notifications (no marketing spam).

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal data to any third party.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not store payment information — Zephyra is free.

For questions, write to us at hello@zephyra.in.

Terms of Service

Last updated: January 2026

By using Zephyra, you agree to these terms. Please read them — they're short, we promise.

Eligibility

  • Zephyra is intended for students aged 13 and above.
  • Users under 18 should have parental or guardian awareness of their participation.

User Conduct

  • You agree to submit honest eco logs — don't fake challenge completions.
  • You will not upload offensive, harmful, or misleading content.
  • You will treat other users and the community with respect.

Our Rights

  • We reserve the right to suspend or remove accounts that violate these terms.
  • Zephyra content (challenges, lessons, design) is the intellectual property of the Zephyra team.

This is a student project provided in good faith. We are not legally liable for technical downtime or data errors, though we will always do our best to resolve issues promptly.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Zephyra uses a minimal number of cookies to keep the platform functional and improve your experience.

Cookies We Use

  • Session cookies: Keep you logged in during your visit. These expire when you close your browser.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings (e.g. dark mode, language). Stored for up to 30 days.
  • Analytics cookies: Anonymous usage data via a privacy-respecting analytics tool. No personal identifiers are tracked.

What We Don't Use

  • No third-party advertising cookies.
  • No social media tracking pixels.
  • No cross-site tracking of any kind.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as expected.
